About

I design and build human–machine interfaces for cars — digital instrument clusters, in-vehicle infotainment and connected cockpit experiences. My work sits between design and engineering: I create interface design systems in Figma, build interactive prototypes, and develop working software, from HMI interface themes to utility mini-programs and automation tools.

What I Do

HMI Interface Design

Instrument clusters and infotainment interfaces for production-oriented automotive projects, including full-width 5120×1440 cockpit displays and 1920×532 cluster layouts.

Design Systems & UI Kits

Reusable component libraries, gauge and dial systems, and editable Figma templates for automotive interfaces.

Prototyping & Development

Turning designs into working software: interactive prototypes, web-based 3D spaces, and front-end implementations of interface concepts.

Mini-Programs & Tools

WeChat mini-programs and small tools — including an automatic decoration quotation calculator and media utility apps — designed, built and maintained end to end.

Selected Work

Car Instrument Cluster UI

A detailed 1920×532 digital cluster interface with layered gauges, drive modes and theme variants, built as a fully editable design system.

BotForge

A virtual 3D office populated by AI agent characters — an experiment in giving software agents a shared, visible workspace.

Decoration Quote Mini-Program

A WeChat mini-program that turns construction drawings into itemised renovation quotations automatically.

Daily Motion Sketches

A daily short-video practice exploring generative scenery, motion and sound — produced with an automated pipeline I built.
